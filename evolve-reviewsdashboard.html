Designing anAI-driven Reviews Insights Dashboard

Overview

Evolve is a vacation rental management company focused on delivering 5‑star guest experiences and management services for vacation rental owners. Most of Evolve's owner users fall within the 45–64 age range and are distributed across the US.

Problem

Owners often struggle to keep up with review volume and identify patterns that impact guest satisfaction. Feedback is scattered, subjective, and time-consuming to interpret — especially for owners managing multiple properties or those who aren’t hands-on.

Solution

The solution was to build an AI-driven reviews dashboard that celebrates what guests love, highlights what to improve, and helps owners grow with confidence. Deliver personalized, simple insights and recommendations like:
💬 “Your guests frequently mention check-in ease — a key strength.”
💬 “Cleanliness mentions dropped this month; consider a mid-stay inspection.”

Outcome

Our team placed 2nd in the hackathon overall! We received positive feedback from the company and the hackathon judges. The next step was to discuss how to make this a go-to-market product.
